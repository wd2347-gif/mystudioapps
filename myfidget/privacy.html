Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026 · App: MyFidget · Operator: MyStudio · Contact support@mystudioapps.com

MyFidget (the "App") is a collection of fidget toys you play with on your device. It works without an account, and the App itself collects no personal information. This policy explains the little data the App handles and where it goes.

1. Data that stays on your device

  • Settings (language, sound and background music, night mode), favourites and the toy styles you chose
  • Simple play counts shown in the App (for example today's taps)
  • Whether Premium is unlocked

This data is stored only on your device and is not sent to the operator or anyone else. Deleting the App deletes it (your device's own backup, such as iCloud, may include it).

2. Purchases — Apple, Google and RevenueCat

Premium is a one-time purchase handled by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play. Your payment details go to the store, not to us. To confirm and restore the purchase, the App uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA):

RecipientCountryDataPurposeWhen / howRetention
RevenueCat, Inc.USAAn anonymous ID created for the purchase, the App Store or Google Play receipt (product, date, store country and currency), device model, OS and app version, IP addressTo confirm and restore your Premium purchaseWhen you open the purchase screen, buy or restore — sent over an encrypted connectionWhile needed for purchase records, or until you ask us to delete it

This is a transfer of personal information to another country. If you do not want it, do not buy or restore Premium — the free toys work without it. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

3. What the App does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs, and no ads
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA / Android advertising ID) and no tracking across apps or websites
  • No access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location
  • We do not sell or share personal information

4. Children

The App is for everyone and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases go through the store account, where parents can require approval (for example Ask to Buy).

5. Your rights and contact

You can ask us to access, correct or delete data related to your purchase, or to stop processing it. Contact support@mystudioapps.com. We answer within 10 days.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If what the App sends changes, we will update this policy first.

MyFidget(以下「本アプリ」)は、端末で遊ぶフィジェットおもちゃのコレクションです。アカウントなしで使え、本アプリ自体は個人情報を集めません。このポリシーは、本アプリが扱うわずかな情報とその行き先を説明します。

これらは端末だけに保存され、運営者や第三者には送られません。本アプリを削除すると一緒に消えます(iCloud など端末のバックアップには残ることがあります)。
