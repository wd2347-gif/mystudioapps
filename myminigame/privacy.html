Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026 · App: MyPocketGames · Operator: MyStudio · Contact support@mystudioapps.com

MyPocketGames (the "App") is a collection of mini games you play on your own: quick-reflex arcade games, puzzles, brain games, and board and card games. It works without an account. The App itself keeps your data on your device, and your scores and best records never leave it. Only the purchase service, the ad service (free version only) and our usage-statistics and crash-report service receive some data, and the App reads a list of our other apps from our website (section 2).

1. Data that stays on your device

  • Your best record in each game, which is also shown on the game's card
  • Your Marbles balance (the App's in-app currency), the games you opened with Marbles, and the date you last received the first-round-of-the-day bonus
  • Whether Premium is unlocked, whether the Premium gift Marbles have been added, and which Marble packs have already been added
  • Favourites and settings: language, dark mode, sound on or off and volume, vibration on or off
  • A saved copy of the list of our other apps used for our own ads (section 2)

This data is stored only on your device and is not sent to the operator or anyone else. Deleting the App deletes all of it, including your records, your Marbles balance and the games you opened (your device's own backup, such as iCloud, may include it). If you reinstall the App and restore your purchases, the Premium gift Marbles and the Marble packs you bought may be given again (Terms of Use, section 2).

2. Data sent to third parties: purchases, ads and usage statistics

Purchases: Apple, Google and RevenueCat

The App offers two in-app purchases, handled by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play: Premium (a one-time purchase) and Marble packs (a consumable purchase you can buy more than once). Your payment details go to the store, not to us. To confirm purchases, add the Marbles you bought and restore Premium, the App uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA):

If you do not want this, you can stop using the App and ask us to delete the data (support@mystudioapps.com). RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Ads: Google AdMob

The free version shows ads: a banner on the home screen and under some games, and sometimes a full-screen ad when you open a game. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC, USA), which receives:

  • Device and app information (model, OS and app version, language), IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred)
  • How you interact with ads (impressions, taps) and diagnostic data from the ad SDK
  • The device advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, advertising ID on Android): on iPhone only if you allow tracking

Google uses this to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Ads are personalised only if you allow it. On iPhone, the App asks whether you allow tracking after you finish your first game, and you can change your answer any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In the EEA and the UK, a consent screen appears first, and you can change your choice any time with "Ad privacy settings" at the bottom of the purchase screen. If you do not allow it, ads are not personalised. Premium removes ads. See how Google uses information from apps.

Our own ads: the MyStudio app list and playable demos

Some ad spaces show ads for our other apps instead of Google ads. For this the App downloads a list of our apps, with their icons and screenshots, from our website (mystudioapps.com/xpromo/feed.json). Some of these ads may be short playable demos of our other apps: web pages from our website (mystudioapps.com/playable/) shown inside the ad. The App tells a demo page only the language and that it is being shown in MyPocketGames; the demo pages load nothing from other companies and collect no personal data. All of these are read-only downloads: the App sends no personal data with them. Like any web request they carry your IP address, which our hosting provider (GitHub Pages, GitHub, Inc., USA) may keep in its server logs for security; we do not receive it. If you tap one of these ads, the App Store or Google Play opens with a tag that shows which of our apps the tap came from, so we can count taps.

Usage statistics and crash reports: Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics

To see which games are played and to fix problems, the App sends the following to Google Firebase (Google LLC, USA):

  • Usage events: which screens and games you open, how long you stay and how many rounds you finish, when a round ends and how many Marbles it gives, whether the purchase screen was viewed, Premium bought or restored, a Marble pack bought or a game opened with Marbles (no payment details), and whether ads loaded and our own ads were shown, closed or tapped
  • Crash reports: the error, the app's state when it happened, device model, OS and app version
  • A random app-instance identifier created by Firebase (not the advertising identifier), language, and an approximate country from the IP address

Your scores, best records and anything you type in the App, such as a game search, are never sent. This data is not linked to your name or contact details and is not used for ads. Google keeps analytics data for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days. This is a transfer of personal information to another country; if you do not want it, you can stop using the App and ask us to delete it (support@mystudioapps.com). Firebase privacy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

3. What the App does not do

  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites unless you allow it (iPhone tracking prompt)
  • No access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location. The games are played by touch and do not use the motion sensor
  • No chat and no contact with other people: every game is played on your own, against the game or the computer
  • Your scores and best records never leave your device
  • We do not sell personal information

4. Children

The App is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It is single-player, with no chat and no way to contact other people. Ads are not personalised unless tracking or consent is allowed. Purchases go through the store account, where parents can require approval (for example Ask to Buy).

5. Your rights and contact

You can ask us to access, correct or delete data related to your purchases or usage statistics, or to stop processing it. Data kept only on your device (section 1) is deleted when you delete the App. Contact support@mystudioapps.com. We answer within 10 days.

US state residents

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, such as California, you have the right to know about and access the personal information about you, to delete or correct it, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. If you allow personalised ads, the advertising identifier and other data sent to Google AdMob may count as "sharing" or targeted advertising under these laws. To opt out, on iPhone do not allow tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; on Android, delete your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads. If the App shows Ad privacy settings, you can also opt out there. For any other request, write to support@mystudioapps.com. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If what the App sends changes, we will update this policy first.

本アプリには、App Store(Apple)または Google Play が処理するアプリ内課金が2種類あります：プレミアム(買い切り)とビー玉パック(何度でも買える消耗型)。支払い情報はストアに送られ、運営者は受け取りません。購入の確認、購入したビー玉の追加、プレミアムの復元のために RevenueCat(RevenueCat, Inc.、米国)を使います。

提供先国項目目的時期・方法保存期間
RevenueCat, Inc.米国購入用に作られる匿名 ID、App Store・Google Play のレシートと購入記録(商品・日付・ストアの国と通貨)、端末の機種・OS・アプリのバージョン、IP アドレスプレミアム・ビー玉パックの購入確認、購入したビー玉の追加、プレミアムの復元本アプリの起動時、購入画面を開いたとき、購入・復元のときに暗号化された通信で購入記録に必要な間、または削除のご依頼まで

これは個人情報の国外移転です。本アプリは起動時に購入状態を確認するため、何も購入しなくても匿名 ID と端末の情報は送られます(支払い情報は含みません)。望まない場合は本アプリの利用をやめ、削除を求めることができます(support@mystudioapps.com)。RevenueCat のプライバシーポリシー：revenuecat.com/privacy

広告：Google AdMob

無料版には広告が表示されます(ホームと一部のゲームの下のバナー、ゲームを開くときにときどき全画面広告)。広告は Google AdMob(Google LLC、米国)が提供し、次の情報を受け取ります。

  • 端末・アプリの情報(機種、OS・アプリのバージョン、言語)、IP アドレス(おおよその位置が推定されることがあります)
  • 広告の表示・タップなどの利用状況と広告 SDK の診断情報
  • 端末の広告識別子(iPhone の IDFA、Android の広告 ID)：iPhone ではトラッキングを許可した場合のみ

Google はこれらを広告の表示・測定と不正防止に使います。パーソナライズ広告は許可した場合のみです。iPhone では最初のゲームを終えたあとにトラッキングの許可を確認し、設定 › プライバシーとセキュリティ › トラッキング でいつでも変更できます。EEA・英国では先に同意画面が表示され、購入画面の下の「広告のプライバシー設定」でいつでも変更できます。許可しない場合はパーソナライズされない広告が表示されます。プレミアムで広告は表示されなくなります。Google によるアプリ情報の使い方

自社広告：MyStudio のアプリ一覧と体験広告

一部の広告枠には、Google の広告の代わりに MyStudio のほかのアプリの広告が表示されます。そのために本アプリは、運営者のウェブサイト(mystudioapps.com/xpromo/feed.json)からアプリの一覧とアイコン・スクリーンショットを読み込みます。これらの広告の一部は、ほかのアプリを少し試せる体験広告の場合があります：運営者のウェブサイト(mystudioapps.com/playable/)のウェブページを広告の中に表示するものです。本アプリはこのページに言語と MyPocketGames から表示したことだけを伝え、体験ページはほかの会社のものを読み込まず、個人情報を集めません。いずれも読み込むだけで、個人情報は送りません。通常のインターネット通信と同じく IP アドレスは伝わり、サイトをホストする GitHub Pages(GitHub, Inc.、米国)がセキュリティのためにサーバーの記録に残すことがあります。運営者はこれを受け取りません。この広告をタップすると App Store または Google Play が開き、どのアプリからタップされたかを数えるための印が付きます。

利用統計とクラッシュレポート：Firebase Analytics・Crashlytics

どのゲームがよく遊ばれているかを知り、不具合を直すために、次の情報を Google Firebase(Google LLC、米国)に送ります。

  • 利用の記録：どの画面・ゲームを開き、どのくらい使い、何回遊び終えたか、1回が終わったときともらったビー玉、購入画面を見たか・プレミアムの購入や復元・ビー玉パックの購入・ビー玉でのゲームの開放をしたか(支払い情報は含みません)、広告を読み込めたか・自社広告が表示されたか閉じられたかタップされたか
  • クラッシュレポート：エラーの内容、そのときのアプリの状態、端末の機種・OS・アプリのバージョン
  • Firebase が作るランダムなアプリのインストール ID(広告 ID ではありません)、言語、IP アドレスから推定したおおよその国

スコア、ベスト記録、ゲームの検索など本アプリに入力した内容は一切送りません。これらは氏名・連絡先と結び付けず、広告には使いません。Google は統計を最大14か月、クラッシュレポートを90日保存します。これは個人情報の国外移転です。望まない場合は本アプリの利用をやめ、削除を求めることができます(support@mystudioapps.com)。Firebase のプライバシー：firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3. 本アプリがしないこと

  • 許可しない限り、他社のアプリやサイトをまたぐトラッキングはしません(iPhone のトラッキング確認)
  • カメラ・マイク・写真・連絡先・位置情報にアクセスしません。ゲームは画面のタッチで遊び、モーションセンサーは使いません
  • チャットやほかの人と連絡する機能はありません：すべてのゲームはひとりで、ゲームやコンピューターを相手に遊びます
  • スコアやベスト記録は端末の外に出ません
  • 個人情報を販売しません

4. お子さま

本アプリは一般向けで、13歳未満のお子さまを対象としていません。ひとりで遊ぶゲームなので、チャットやほかの人と連絡する機能はありません。トラッキング・同意を許可しない限り、パーソナライズ広告は表示されません。購入はストアのアカウントで行われ、保護者は承認(例：承認と購入のリクエスト)を設定できます。
