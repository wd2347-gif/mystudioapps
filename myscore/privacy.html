Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026 · App: MyScore · Operator: MyStudio · Contact support@mystudioapps.com

MyScore (the "App") places bowing marks on orchestra parts (PDFs or images) that you open on your device. It works without an account, and your score files never leave your device. This policy explains what data the App handles and where it goes.

1. Data that stays on your device

The following data is stored only on your device. It is not sent to the operator or to any third party.

  • Copies of the scores you import (PDFs, or images the App combines into a PDF), and what the App reads from them (positions of staves, measures and notes)
  • Bowing marks, drafts and saved versions
  • Settings (language, and for each score its part, time signature and mark style) and whether Premium is unlocked

The App reads scores and assigns bowings entirely on your device, and never uploads score files to a server. Deleting a score in the App, or deleting the App, deletes this data. The operator cannot access it. Depending on your device settings, your device's own backups (such as iCloud or Google backup) may include it. Files you export go only where you choose in the share sheet (for example Files, Photos or a printer).

2. Data sent to third-party services

Purchases — Apple App Store, Google Play, RevenueCat

Premium is a one-time purchase processed by Apple or Google. The App never handles card details. To confirm and restore your purchase, when the App starts and when you open the purchase screen, purchase receipts and transaction details, an anonymous identifier generated by the App, and basic device and app information (such as OS and app version) are sent to RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). No name, email address or other identity data is included.

Usage statistics — Google Analytics for Firebase

To improve the App, usage events are sent to Google Firebase (Google LLC, USA): the screens you open and the features you use — for example running auto bowing, saving a version or opening the Premium screen — as numbers and categories only (which engine or part, the time signature, how many marks, success or failure). Firebase also records the app version, device model and OS, language, approximate region (derived from your IP address), an app-instance identifier and, on Android, the install source reported by Google Play. When you buy Premium, a purchase event with the product and price may also be recorded.

  • We never send score titles, file names, score contents or your bowing marks.
  • The App does not collect your device's advertising identifier, and this data is not used for advertising or ad personalization.
  • If you turn off Send usage statistics in Settings, nothing more is sent.

Error reports — Firebase Crashlytics

If the App crashes or hits an error, a report is sent to Google Firebase (Google LLC, USA) so we can fix it: where in the App's code it went wrong, the device model and OS, the app version, the screens opened just before, and an installation identifier. Reports contain no score files, titles or bowing marks. They are sent only while Send usage statistics is on.

3. Transfers abroad

These services process data on servers outside Korea, mainly in the United States. Data is sent over encrypted connections while you use the App.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Usage statistics and error reports: turn them on or off at any time in Settings › Send usage statistics.
  • Data on your device: deleted when you delete a score in the App or delete the App.
  • The App has no accounts, and the operator cannot tell who you are from the data it receives, so we cannot look up data by person. For requests to access, delete or stop processing data held by the third-party services, write to the address below and we will help as far as we can.
  • Users in the EU and UK: the legal basis is performance of the purchase contract for confirming purchases (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in improving the App for usage statistics and error reports (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection, and you may lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

5. Permissions the App requests

The App asks for no special permissions up front. It reads only the files and photos you choose in the system picker; it does not access the rest of your photo library, or your camera, microphone, contacts or location. On iOS, if you save exported images to Photos, the system asks for permission to add photos, which the App uses only for that.

6. Retention and deletion

On-device data is kept until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data sent to third-party services is kept under each service's policy; usage statistics are deleted according to the Google Analytics data-retention setting.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

8. Security

Data sent to third-party services travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The operator does not receive information that identifies you (name, email address or phone number).

9. Changes

Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will be announced on this page before they take effect.

プライバシーポリシー

施行日 2026年10月6日 · アプリ: MyScore · 運営者: MyStudio · お問い合わせ support@mystudioapps.com

MyScore（以下「本アプリ」）は、端末で開いたオーケストラのパート譜（PDFまたは画像）にボウイング記号を付けるアプリです。アカウントなしで使え、楽譜ファイルが端末の外に出ることはありません。このポリシーでは、本アプリが扱う情報とその送信先を説明します。

1. 端末内にのみ保存される情報

次の情報は端末内にのみ保存され、運営者や第三者に送信されません。

  • 取り込んだ楽譜のコピー（PDF、または本アプリが画像をまとめたPDF）と、本アプリがそこから読み取った情報（五線・小節・音符の位置）
  • ボウイング記号、下書き、保存したバージョン
  • 設定（言語、および楽譜ごとのパート・拍子・記号のスタイル）とプレミアムの利用状況

楽譜の読み取りとボウイングの配分はすべて端末内で行い、楽譜ファイルをサーバーにアップロードすることはありません。アプリで楽譜を削除するか、アプリを削除すると、これらの情報も削除されます。運営者がこれらの情報を閲覧することはできません。ただし、端末の設定によっては、端末自体のバックアップ（iCloud、Googleバックアップなど）に含まれる場合があります。書き出したファイルは、利用者が共有画面で選んだ場所（ファイル・写真・印刷など）にだけ送られます。

2. 第三者サービスに送信される情報

購入 — Apple App Store、Google Play、RevenueCat

プレミアムは、AppleまたはGoogleの決済システムで処理される買い切りの購入です。本アプリがカード情報を扱うことはありません。購入の確認と復元のため、アプリの起動時と購入画面を開いたときに、購入レシートと取引情報、本アプリが生成した匿名の識別子、端末とアプリの情報（OS、アプリのバージョンなど）がRevenueCat（RevenueCat, Inc.、米国）に送信されます。氏名やメールアドレスなどの個人を特定する情報は含まれません。

利用統計 — Google Analytics for Firebase

本アプリの改善のため、利用イベントをGoogle Firebase（Google LLC、米国）に送信します。開いた画面と使った機能（例：自動ボウイングの実行、バージョンの保存、プレミアム画面を開く）を、数値と分類だけで送ります（どのエンジン・パートか、拍子、記号の数、成功したかなど）。Firebaseはあわせて、アプリのバージョン、端末の機種とOS、言語、おおよその地域（IPアドレスから推定）、アプリのインスタンス識別子、AndroidではGoogle Playが知らせるインストール経路を記録します。プレミアムを購入すると、商品と価格を含む購入イベントも記録される場合があります。

  • 楽譜のタイトル、ファイル名、楽譜の内容、ボウイング記号は送信しません。
  • 端末の広告識別子は収集せず、これらの情報を広告やパーソナライズ広告に使うことはありません。
  • 設定の利用統計を送信をオフにすると、以後は送信しません。

エラー報告 — Firebase Crashlytics

本アプリが停止したりエラーが起きたりすると、修正のために報告がGoogle Firebase（Google LLC、米国）に送信されます。アプリのコードのどこで問題が起きたか、端末の機種とOS、アプリのバージョン、直前に開いた画面、インストールの識別子が含まれ、楽譜ファイル・タイトル・ボウイング記号は含まれません。設定の利用統計を送信がオンのときだけ送信します。
