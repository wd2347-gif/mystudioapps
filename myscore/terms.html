1. Service

MyScore (the "App") reads orchestra parts (PDFs or images) that you open on your device, automatically assigns bowings (down-bow ⊓ / up-bow ∨) and shows them as an overlay on the original. Core features, including auto bowing with the Light engine, are free. Some features are offered through Premium, a yearly subscription.

2. Automatic bowing and its limits

Bowings are assigned automatically from what the App recognizes in the score, and both recognition and assignment can be wrong. Passages the App could not read reliably are drawn faintly and flagged for review. Check the bowings yourself before rehearsals and performances. The operator does not guarantee that automatic results are accurate or suitable for any particular performance.

3. Premium (yearly subscription)

  • Premium unlocks the Advanced engine, Manual bowing, Mark style and Export (PDF, JPG, PNG). It is an auto-renewing yearly subscription at the price shown in the App and the store.
  • If a free trial is offered, the yearly price is charged when the trial ends unless you cancel before then. The store decides whether you can receive a free trial (usually once per account).
  • The subscription renews automatically every year at the same price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends, and payment is charged to your Apple App Store or Google Play account. You can cancel at any time in your store account's subscription settings; after cancelling, Premium stays available until the end of the period you paid for. If the price changes, the store notifies you in advance under its policy.
  • When the subscription ends, the bowings you already made stay in your scores; only the Premium features lock again.
  • Subscriptions belong to the store account that made them. After reinstalling the App or switching devices, tap Restore purchase with the same store account.
  • Refunds follow each store's policy. The operator cannot refund store purchases directly.

  • Files you export (PDF, JPG or PNG) contain the score together with your bowings. Use them for your own practice and performance, and don't distribute them without the rights holder's permission.
  • You are responsible for how you use score files. The operator is not responsible for infringement arising from files you import.

  • When you view or export an ensemble score, the ensemble name and your name appear on each page. Exported ensemble scores are for your own practice and performance, like any export (section 4).
