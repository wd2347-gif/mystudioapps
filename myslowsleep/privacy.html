Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026 · App: MySlowSleep · Operator: MyStudio · Contact support@mystudioapps.com

MySlowSleep (the "App") helps you fall asleep with sounds, a sleep timer and breathing guidance. It works without an account and never asks for your name or email. The App is free and shows ads; MySlowSleep Premium removes all ads and unlocks volume control for each sound and breathing. Your settings stay on your device (section 1). Only the companies below receive some data, for usage statistics and crash reports, purchase checks and ads in the free version, and the App reads a list of other MyStudio apps from our website (section 2).

1. Data that stays on your device

  • Settings: sound levels, the sounds you chose, color, sleep timer and breathing settings, bedtime reminder settings (on or off and the time), and whether you have seen the first-launch guide
  • Values your home-screen widget reads (whether sound is playing, levels, sound, timer and color): kept in on-device storage shared with the widget (iOS App Group, Android widget storage)
  • Premium status: whether a subscription, Lifetime or the earlier paid version is active
  • A saved copy of the list of other MyStudio apps used for our own ads (section 2)

This data is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers or anyone else. Deleting the App deletes it (a device backup, such as iCloud or your Google account backup, may still include it). Premium can be brought back with "Restore purchases" (see the Terms of Use).

Usage statistics and crash reports: Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics

To fix errors and see which features are used, the App (store releases only) sends the following to Google Firebase (Google LLC, USA):

  • Usage events: the screens and panels you open (home, breathing, color and Premium), sounds you turn on or off (the nature sounds and music you chose, frequency on/off, timer minutes and minutes played), breathing sessions (rhythm, rounds, voice guide on/off), color changes, home-screen widget buttons, whether you allowed bedtime reminders and opened the App from one, and update and rating prompts
  • Premium and ads: whether you opened the Premium screen, tapped a plan, bought or restored (the product and its price, never payment details); whether ads loaded and were shown, with estimated revenue; whether our own ads were shown or tapped
  • Crash reports: the error and the app's state when it happened
  • A random app-instance ID created by Firebase, device model, OS and app version, language, an approximate region from the IP address, and the install source (Android only: which store link or campaign the App was installed from)

On iPhone, if you allow tracking, Firebase Analytics may also receive the advertising identifier (IDFA); on Android it does not collect the advertising ID. We do not collect your name, email or precise location, and this data is not linked to your contact details, used to personalise ads or sold. Google keeps analytics data for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days. This is a transfer of personal information to another country. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Premium: in-app purchases with Apple, Google

The App is free. If you like, you can buy MySlowSleep Premium: all ads disappear, and volume control for each sound (nature sounds, frequency and music separately) and breathing are unlocked. It is available as a monthly or yearly subscription that renews automatically, or as a one-time Lifetime purchase. The price is shown in the App and in the store before you buy.

If you bought the App when it was a paid app, every feature stays unlocked without buying Premium. To check this, the App looks for settings the earlier version saved on your device, and on iPhone also at the first version recorded in your App Store receipt (through RevenueCat). Nothing extra is collected for this.

Payment is handled by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). Payment details such as your card number go to the store; neither the App nor we receive them.

Purchase checks and ad revenue: RevenueCat

To confirm Premium and restore it on a new device or after reinstalling, and to add up ad revenue together with purchases, the App uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created for purchases, App Store and Google Play receipts and purchase records (product, date, store country and currency), records of ads loaded, shown and tapped with estimated ad revenue (ad company and ad space), device model, OS and app version, and IP address. Because the App checks Premium when it starts, the anonymous ID and device information are sent even if you never buy anything (no payment details). This is a transfer of personal information to another country, used only to confirm and restore purchases and to add up ad revenue. You can ask us to delete it (support@mystudioapps.com). RevenueCat privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Bedtime reminders

If you turn on bedtime reminders, the App schedules them on your device and your device's own notification system shows them. The reminder text and time are not sent anywhere; usage statistics only record whether you allowed reminders and whether you opened the App from one.

3. How long data is kept

  • Data on your device: until you delete the App
  • Firebase Analytics: up to 14 months; Crashlytics: 90 days
  • RevenueCat: as long as needed for purchase and ad revenue records, or until you ask us to delete it
  • Apple and Google (stores), Unity LevelPlay, ironSource Ads and the other ad companies, GitHub: under each company's privacy policy

4. Permissions the App uses

  • Background audio (iOS): so sounds keep playing with the screen off or locked.
  • Notifications (iOS; Android: POST_NOTIFICATIONS, VIBRATE): for bedtime reminders. The App asks only after you choose Get reminders.
  • Tracking (iOS): the App asks once; ads are personalised only if you allow it (section 2).
  • Internet and network state (Android: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to send usage statistics and crash reports, check Premium, and load ads and our app list (section 2).
  • In-app purchases (Android: BILLING): to buy and restore Premium through Google Play.
  • Advertising ID (Android: AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): for the ads in the free version (section 2).
  • Install source (Android: BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE): to record which store link or campaign the App was installed from (section 2).
  • Start after a restart (Android: RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): to set bedtime reminders again after the device restarts or the App updates, and to send reports that could not be sent earlier.
  • Keep awake and media playback service (Android: WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): so sounds keep playing on the lock screen and the sleep timer can fade them out.

5. What the App does not do

  • No account or sign-in, and no server of ours that stores your data
  • It never asks for your name, email or phone number
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites unless you allow it (iPhone tracking prompt)
  • No access to your microphone, camera, photos, contacts, location or health data
  • We do not sell personal information

6. Children

The App is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are not personalised unless tracking or consent is allowed. Purchases go through the store account, and parents can block or approve them with features such as Ask to Buy (Apple) or purchase approvals (Google Play).

購入の確認と広告収益の集計：RevenueCat

プレミアムを確認し、新しい端末や再インストール後に復元するため、また広告収益を購入とあわせて集計するために RevenueCat(RevenueCat, Inc.、米国)を使います。RevenueCat は、購入用に作られる匿名 ID、App Store・Google Play のレシートと購入記録(商品・日付・ストアの国と通貨)、広告の読み込み・表示・タップの記録と広告収益の推定額(広告ネットワーク・広告枠)、端末の機種・OS・アプリのバージョン、IP アドレスを受け取ります。本アプリは起動時にプレミアムの状態を確認するため、何も購入しなくても匿名 ID と端末の情報は送られます(支払い情報は含みません)。これは個人情報の国外移転で、購入の確認・復元と広告収益の集計のためだけに使われます。削除を求めることができます(support@mystudioapps.com)。RevenueCat のプライバシーポリシー: revenuecat.com/privacy

広告：Unity LevelPlay・ironSource Ads

無料版には広告が表示されます。広告は Unity LevelPlay と ironSource Ads(Unity Technologies、米国、および関連会社の ironSource、イスラエル)を通じて表示され、これらが次の情報を受け取ります。

  • 端末・アプリの情報(機種、OS・アプリのバージョン、言語)、IP アドレス(おおよその位置が推定されることがあります)
  • 広告の表示・タップなどの利用状況、広告 SDK の診断情報、Android では端末にインストールされているほかのアプリの情報とアプリセット ID(同じ開発者のアプリの間だけで使われる識別子で、統計・不正防止に使われます)
  • 本アプリが LevelPlay にユーザー ID として渡す購入用の匿名 ID(RevenueCat が作るもので、広告収益を同じ ID で照らし合わせるために使われます)
  • 端末の広告識別子：iPhone の IDFA はトラッキングを許可した場合のみ、Android は広告 ID

これらの事業者は、この情報を広告の選択・表示・測定と不正防止に使います。広告を適切なタイミングで表示できるよう、本アプリの起動時にも広告 SDK が端末・アプリの情報を受け取ることがあります。プレミアム(サブスクリプション・買い切り・以前の有料版の購入)なら、本アプリは広告を読み込みません。パーソナライズ広告は許可した場合のみです：iPhone ではトラッキングの確認と 設定 › プライバシーとセキュリティ › トラッキング で、EEA・英国・スイスでは本アプリが表示する同意画面(Google User Messaging Platform、Google LLC、米国)で選び、選んだ内容は LevelPlay に渡されます。プレミアム画面の下に広告のプライバシー設定が表示される場合は、そこでいつでも変更できます。Android では 設定 › Google › 広告 で広告 ID を削除またはリセットできます。許可しない場合はパーソナライズされない広告が表示されます。これも個人情報の国外移転です。Unity のプライバシーポリシー

その他の広告ネットワーク：iPhone では、Unity LevelPlay は広告枠を埋めるため、以下の広告ネットワークにも広告を要求する場合があります。これらの会社も同じ目的で同じ種類の情報(端末・アプリの情報、IP アドレス(おおよその位置)、広告の表示・タップ、広告識別子(トラッキングを許可した場合のみ))を受け取ります。Unity Ads(Unity Technologies、米国): unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy。Liftoff(Vungle, Inc.、米国): liftoff.io/privacy-policy。Meta Audience Network(Meta Platforms, Inc.、米国・Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.): facebook.com/privacy/policy。Mintegral(Mintegral International Ltd.、シンガポール、中国 Mobvista の子会社): mintegral.com/en/privacy。Google AdMob(Google LLC、米国): policies.google.com/privacy。これも個人情報の国外移転です。

自社広告：MyStudio のアプリ一覧

一部の広告枠には、MyStudio のほかのアプリの広告が表示されます。そのために本アプリは、運営者のウェブサイト(mystudioapps.com/xpromo/feed.json)からアプリの一覧とアイコンを読み込みます。読み込むだけで、個人情報は送りません。通常のインターネット通信と同じく IP アドレスは伝わり、サイトをホストする GitHub Pages(GitHub, Inc.、米国)がセキュリティのためにサーバーの記録に残すことがあります。運営者はこれを受け取りません。この広告をタップすると App Store または Google Play が開き、どのアプリからタップされたかを数えるための印が付きます。

4. 本アプリが使う権限

  • バックグラウンドオーディオ(iOS)：画面を消したりロックしたりしても音が続くようにするためです。
  • 通知(iOS、Android: POST_NOTIFICATIONS、VIBRATE)：おやすみ通知のためです。「通知を受け取る」を選んだあとにだけ確認します。
  • トラッキング(iOS)：一度だけ確認し、許可した場合のみパーソナライズ広告が表示されます(第2章)。
  • インターネット・ネットワーク状態(Android: INTERNET、ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)：利用統計・クラッシュレポートを送り、プレミアムを確認し、広告と MyStudio のアプリ一覧を読み込むためです(第2章)。
  • アプリ内課金(Android: BILLING)：Google Play でプレミアムを購入・復元するためです。
  • 広告 ID(Android: AD_ID、ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID、ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION、ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS)：無料版の広告のためです(第2章)。
  • インストール経路(Android: BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE)：どのストアのリンク・キャンペーンからインストールしたかを記録するためです(第2章)。
  • 再起動後の実行(Android: RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)：端末の再起動や本アプリのアップデートのあと、おやすみ通知をもう一度設定し、送れなかった記録を送るためです。
  • スリープ防止・メディア再生サービス(Android: WAKE_LOCK、FOREGROUND_SERVICE、FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK)：ロック画面でも音が続き、スリープタイマーが音をゆっくり小さくして止められるようにするためです。
