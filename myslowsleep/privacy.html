Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026 · App: MySlowSleep · Operator: MyStudio · Contact support@mystudioapps.com

MySlowSleep (the "App") helps you fall asleep with sounds, a sleep timer and breathing guidance. It works without an account and never asks for your name or email. Settings stay on your device; to fix errors and improve the App, it sends usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase (section 3).

1. Data that stays on your device

  • Settings: sound levels, the sounds you chose, color, sleep timer and breathing settings
  • Values your home-screen widget reads (whether sound is playing, levels, sound, timer and color): kept in on-device storage shared with the widget (iOS App Group, Android widget storage)

This data is stored only on your device and is not sent to the operator or anyone else. Deleting the App deletes it (your device's own backup, such as iCloud, may include it).

2. Purchases

The App is sold through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play. Payment is handled directly by the store: the App and the operator never receive your payment details.

3. What the App does not do

  • No advertising SDKs, and no ads
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA / Android advertising ID) and no tracking across apps or websites
  • No access to your microphone, camera, photos, contacts, location or health data
  • We do not sell or share personal information

Usage statistics and crash reports: Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics

To fix errors and see which features are used, the App (store releases only) sends the following to Google Firebase (Google LLC, USA):

  • Usage events: the screens you open (home, breathing and color panels), sounds you turn on or off (the nature sounds and music you chose, frequency on/off, timer minutes and minutes played), breathing sessions (rhythm, rounds, voice guide on/off), color changes and home-screen widget buttons
  • Crash reports: the error and the app's state when it happened
  • A random app-instance ID created by Firebase, device model, OS and app version, language, and an approximate region from the IP address
  • Install source (Android only: which store link or campaign the App was installed from)

We do not collect your name, email, advertising identifier or precise location, and this data is not linked to your contact details, used for advertising or sold. Google keeps analytics data for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Permissions the App uses

  • Background audio (iOS): so sounds keep playing with the screen off or locked.
  • Internet and network state (Android: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): to send the usage statistics and crash reports in section 3 (after a restart, reports that could not be sent are sent later).
  • Keep awake and media playback service (Android: WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): so sounds keep playing on the lock screen and the sleep timer can fade them out.

5. Children

The App is for everyone and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases go through the store account, where parents can require approval (for example Ask to Buy).

6. Your rights and contact

Settings stay on your device and are deleted with the App. To ask about or delete usage statistics linked to your app-instance ID, or for any question about this policy, contact support@mystudioapps.com. We answer within 10 days.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If what the App sends changes, we will update this policy first.
