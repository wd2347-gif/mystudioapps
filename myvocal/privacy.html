Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026 · App: MyVocal · Operator: MyStudio · Contact support@mystudioapps.com

MyVocal (the "App") helps you practise the basics of singing: warm-ups, breathing, vocal scales and ear training. It works without an account. The App itself keeps your data on your device, and microphone sound is analysed on your device only: it is never recorded or sent (section 2). Only the purchase service, the ad service (free version only) and our usage-statistics and crash-report service receive some data (section 3).

1. Data that stays on your device

  • Settings: language, night mode, the voice type you chose or the vocal range you measured, how notes are named (C D E or Do Re Mi), scale options (piano melody on or off, going back down) and piano volume
  • Practice records shown in the App: practice time per day for the last 90 days, which areas you finished today, and your best records (longest "Sss", Pitch Match score, Interval Quiz score, scale accuracy)
  • Whether Premium has been purchased

This data is stored only on your device and is not sent to the operator or anyone else. Deleting the App deletes it (your device's own backup, such as iCloud, may include it).

2. Microphone

Exercises that show your pitch or time your breath (for example Live Pitch and the long "Sss") use the microphone. The App asks for permission when you first open such an exercise.

  • The sound is analysed in real time on your device, only to estimate pitch and loudness.
  • It is never recorded, stored or sent: not to us and not to anyone else.
  • You can turn the microphone off in the exercise, or turn off access in your device settings. The App still works without it; it just cannot show your pitch line or measure your voice.

3. Data sent to third parties: purchases, ads and usage statistics

Purchases: Apple, Google and RevenueCat

Warm-ups, breathing, ear training and the basic vocal exercises are free. "MyVocal Premium (lifetime)" unlocks the rest of the exercises and settings and removes ads. It is a one-time purchase handled by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play. Your payment details go to the store, not to us.   • How you interact with ads (impressions, taps) and diagnostic data from the ad SDK
  • The device advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, advertising ID on Android): on iPhone only if you allow tracking

To see which exercises are used and to fix problems, the App sends the following to Google Firebase (Google LLC, USA):

  • Usage events: which screens and exercises you open and for how long, and whether the Premium screen was viewed and the purchase made or restored (no payment details)
  • Crash reports: the error, the app's state when it happened, device model, OS and app version
  • A random app-instance identifier created by Firebase (not the advertising identifier), language, and an approximate country from the IP address

Microphone sound is never part of this. This data is not linked to your name or contact details and is not used for ads. Google keeps analytics data for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days. This is a transfer of personal information to another country; if you do not want it, you can stop using the App and ask us to delete it (support@mystudioapps.com). Firebase privacy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. What the App does not do

  • No account or sign-in, and no server of our own
  • No recording: microphone sound is never recorded, stored or sent
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites unless you allow it (iPhone tracking prompt)
  • No access to your camera, photos, contacts or location
  • We do not sell personal information

5. Children

The App is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are not personalised unless tracking or consent is allowed. Purchases go through the store account, where parents can require approval (for example Ask to Buy).

6. Your rights and contact

You can ask us to access, correct or delete data related to your purchase, or to stop processing it. Your settings and practice records stay on your device and never reach us; deleting the App removes them. Contact support@mystudioapps.com. We answer within 10 days.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If what the App sends changes, we will update this policy first.

プライバシーポリシー

施行日 2026年10月9日 · アプリ: MyVocal · 運営者: MyStudio · お問い合わせ support@mystudioapps.com

MyVocal(以下「本アプリ」)は、ウォームアップ・呼吸・発声・音感など、歌の基礎を練習するアプリです。アカウントなしで使え、本アプリ自体は情報を端末だけに保存します。マイクの音は端末の中だけで分析し、録音や送信はしません(第2章)。購入確認のサービス、無料版の場合の広告サービス、そして利用統計・クラッシュレポートのサービスだけが一部の情報を受け取ります(第3章)。

1. 端末だけに残る情報

  • 設定: 言語、ナイトモード、選んだ声のタイプまたは測定した声域、音名の表記(C D E・ドレミ)、スケールのオプション(ピアノのメロディのオン・オフ、上がってまた下りる)、ピアノの音量
  • アプリに表示される練習記録: 直近90日間の1日ごとの練習時間、今日終えた項目、自己ベスト(ロング「スー」・音に合わせて歌うのスコア・音程クイズのスコア・スケールの正確さ)
  • プレミアムを購入したかどうか

これらは端末だけに保存され、運営者や第三者には送られません。本アプリを削除すると一緒に消えます(iCloud など端末のバックアップには残ることがあります)。

2. マイク

自分の音程を表示したり、息の長さを測ったりする練習(例: リアルタイム音程、ロング「スー」測定)ではマイクを使います。そうした練習を初めて開くときに、本アプリが許可を求めます。

  • 音は端末の中でリアルタイムに分析し、音の高さと大きさを推定するだけです。
  • 音は録音・保存・送信しません。運営者にも第三者にも送られません。
  • 練習画面でマイクをオフにしたり、端末の設定でマイクへのアクセスをオフにしたりできます。マイクがなくても本アプリは使えます。自分の音程の線と声の測定が表示されないだけです。
